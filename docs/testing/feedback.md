# Report Tester Feedback

Usability findings are as important as technical defects. A control that works
but cannot be understood or found is a meaningful result.

Send feedback to **Lyreline Support** at
[lyreline.support@icloud.com](mailto:lyreline.support@icloud.com).

Do not email confidential screenshots, passwords, personal information,
authentication data, or sensitive business archives. Use fictional examples or
describe the issue without including protected content.

## Severity

- **BLOCKER** — You cannot continue the primary workflow or significant data
  appears lost.
- **MAJOR** — A feature fails or behaves incorrectly, but another path may exist.
- **MINOR** — The problem is annoying or confusing but does not prevent
  completion.
- **USABILITY** — The feature works technically, but you cannot understand how
  to use it. These findings are extremely important.
- **SUGGESTION** — An enhancement or idea rather than a defect.
