# Lyreline 1.0 Tester Program

Thank you for testing Lyreline. Lyreline is intended to help people capture real
workflows and turn them into reusable procedures without rebuilding every
screenshot and instruction by hand.

Please test both whether the features work and whether the product makes sense
without developer assistance. Confusion, hesitation, and hard-to-find controls
are important findings even when the feature eventually works.

Begin with the standard test before experimenting with a workflow of your own.
Use fictional or non-confidential material throughout testing.

## What good testing looks like

Try to work naturally. Do not study every Help page before beginning—the test
also measures whether Lyreline provides enough guidance at the right time.
Write down where you hesitated, what you expected, and what finally helped.

If a test would expose private business information, substitute harmless sample
content or skip that part. Never send confidential screenshots, passwords,
authentication information, or private archives with feedback.
